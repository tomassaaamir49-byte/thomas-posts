# Design critique — run on every slide and the story before delivering

(Adapted from the "Design Critique" skill on Thomas's laptop.)

Look at each rendered image at phone size and check:

1. **First impression (2 seconds)** — what draws the eye first? On slide 1 it must be the hook headline; the purpose must be clear instantly.
2. **Usability** — can the reader follow it without effort? Swipe cue on slide 1, numbering clear, the checklist slide easy to save.
3. **Visual hierarchy** — reading order right-to-left and top-to-bottom: headline → body → small labels; enough whitespace; nothing crowded, no text touching edges, no single orphan word on its own line in a headline.
4. **Consistency** — same theme and font pairing on all slides and the story; labels, numbers and footer in the same place on every slide.
5. **Accessibility** — strong contrast (dark text on light, white on dark); body text large enough to read on a phone; no broken Arabic letters, missing glyphs or emoji boxes.

Write the result for yourself as a small table: Finding | Severity (High/Medium/Low) | Fix.
Fix every High finding (shorten text, change wording, re-render) before sending. Be specific ("the headline wraps to a lonely word 'الأول'"), not vague.
